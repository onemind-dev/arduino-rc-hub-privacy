Privacy Policy — Arduino RC Hub Last updated: September 21, 2026 Arduino RC Hub respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use the app. 1. Information We Collect Arduino RC Hub does not require you to create an account and does not collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address. 2. Bluetooth Arduino RC Hub uses Bluetooth to communicate with compatible Arduino projects and Bluetooth modules. Bluetooth communication is used solely for controlling your connected Arduino project. The application does not collect or store your Bluetooth communications on our servers. 3. Microphone and Voice Commands Arduino RC Hub can use the device microphone to recognize voice commands. Microphone access is used only when you activate the voice-control feature. Voice recognition may be processed by services provided by the device or operating system. Arduino RC Hub does not intentionally store recordings or voice data on its own servers. 4. Data Storage Arduino RC Hub does not operate its own servers for collecting or storing personal user information. Configuration settings may be stored locally on your device so that your customized commands can be used within the application. 5. Third-Party Services Arduino RC Hub may rely on services provided by the Android operating system or device manufacturer for features such as Bluetooth communication, permissions, and speech recognition. These services may have their own privacy policies. 6. Children's Privacy Arduino RC Hub does not knowingly collect personal information from users. 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application changes or new features are added. Any updated version will be published at this same location. 8.
